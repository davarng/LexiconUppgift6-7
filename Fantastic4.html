"Fantastic Four: First Steps" is set to hit theaters on July 25, 2025. Pedro Pascal stars as Reed Richards / Mister Fantastic, Vanessa Kirby as Sue Storm / Invisible Woman, Joseph Quinn as Johnny Storm / Human Torch, and Ebon Moss-Bachrach as Ben Grimm / The Thing. Produced by Marvel Studios and directed by Matt Shakman. It follows the origin story of Marvel's first family as they navigate their newfound powers and face a cosmic threat to humanity.
